# 🚀 Building Zirak AI — Today's Development Update

Today I'm working on expanding **Zirak AI** beyond a single-model desktop assistant.

I'm currently adding **DeepSeek-R1:1.5B through Ollama**. But the bigger goal is to make Zirak AI a place where users can work with multiple compatible AI models — add models, switch between them, and choose the right model for their hardware and tasks.

## What we're building

- **A multi-model AI workspace:** move beyond being locked to one model.
- **Local AI through Ollama:** make local inference a central part of the experience.
- **Model management:** work toward model discovery, downloads, compatibility guidance, verification, and switching.
- **Productivity tools:** bring chat, document workflows, spreadsheets, notes, and useful everyday tools together.
- **A clear product family:** Free, Professional, Secure, and Enterprise editions, each with distinct capabilities and release requirements.

## What happens next

1. Finish and test the current DeepSeek-R1:1.5B integration.
2. Improve model selection and runtime status.
3. Design the model-management workflow.
4. Continue refining the desktop experience.
5. Publish transparent progress, documentation, and release notes.

This is an active development project. Some items above are goals rather than completed features, and I'll label them clearly as implementation progresses.

**The idea is simple: your AI, your models, your workspace.**
